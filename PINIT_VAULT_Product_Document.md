# PINIT Vault - Detailed Product Document

## Page 1: About Our APK

### 1.1 Product Identity
PINIT Vault is a secure digital vault and document management APK designed to protect personal, professional, and sensitive records through biometric authentication, encrypted storage, smart document processing, and secure sharing.

This application is not a normal file storage app. It is a security-first platform built for users who want to store, verify, manage, and share important documents without exposing their data to unauthorized access or fraud.

### 1.2 Purpose of the APK
The main goal of PINIT Vault is to create a secure environment where documents and identity-related information can be managed safely on mobile devices. The app helps users protect records such as:
- PAN card
- Aadhar or identity documents
- passports and certificates
- resumes and employment documents
- business files and invoices
- personal statements and legal records

### 1.3 Core Problem It Solves
In the modern digital world, users face multiple risks:
- personal documents being stolen or misused
- insecure storage on mobile phones
- weak password-based protection
- unauthorized access to private files
- difficulty in proving ownership and authenticity of uploaded documents
- poor monitoring and tracking of document usage or history

PINIT Vault addresses these real-world problems using a layered security model and document-first workflow.

### 1.4 Why This APK Matters
Our APK is designed for the social and digital reality of today, where identity, documents, work records, and personal files are increasingly stored on smartphones. In this environment, a secure mobile platform is essential for both personal protection and professional trust.

### 1.5 Target Users
The app is useful for:
- professionals who store work records and resumes
- business users managing invoices and certificates
- students and job seekers storing academic and employment documents
- families storing identity and legal records
- anyone who deals with personal or confidential documents

## Page 2: Features We Provide

### 2.1 Biometric Login and Secure Access
The app includes secure biometric authentication using fingerprint and face-based verification. This ensures that access is not based only on a standard password, but on user identity.

This feature helps:
- stop unauthorized logins
- improve convenience for mobile users
- protect sensitive vault contents
- reduce fraud and misuse

### 2.2 User Profile Management
The app provides a user profile section where the user can manage identity details, account status, and application preferences. This is essential for keeping the personal account secure and organized.

Profile features include:
- personal profile information
- account monitoring
- secure access status
- user identity management
- safer session handling

### 2.3 Live Scan Feature
The app supports live document scanning directly from the camera. This allows users to scan physical papers in real time and upload them as digital records.

This is especially useful for:
- identity documents
- PAN cards
- certificates
- resumes
- invoices and forms

### 2.4 Live Video Capture Support
The app also supports live video-based input, which adds a more realistic and modern way to capture identity-related content or verification data. This helps in situations where documentation needs a real-time capture or human verification workflow.

### 2.5 Upload Document Feature
Users can upload documents directly into the platform. Uploaded content becomes part of the secure document vault and can be organized, searched, and tracked.

Supported use cases include:
- PAN card
- Aadhaar or ID cards
- resume and CV upload
- professional certificates
- office documents and invoices
- all personal and legal records

### 2.6 Resume Management
The app includes resume-related functionality for users who want to store and manage their professional profiles. Resume documents can be accessed, tracked, and shared securely.

This is highly relevant for:
- job seekers
- recruiters
- HR workflows
- professional portfolio management

### 2.7 PAN Card and Official Document Support
The app supports PAN card and other official documents as part of its secure document management workflow. These are among the most sensitive identity records and therefore require stronger protection.

### 2.8 All Document Types in One Vault
The app allows users to store all types of documents in a single secure environment, including:
- PAN card
- ID documents
- resumes
- contracts
- invoices
- certificate files
- receipts
- business files
- legal or private paperwork

This is a major convenience feature because it centralizes all records in one protected place.

### 2.9 Secure Sharing of Documents
The app includes document sharing capabilities. Users can share files securely without exposing the entire vault or leaving sensitive information vulnerable.

This feature is useful for:
- sharing work files securely
- sending documents to HR or employers
- sharing official records with trusted parties
- temporary access for approval and review

### 2.10 File Monitoring and Tracking
PINIT Vault includes monitoring and tracking functions so users and administrators can understand document activity and status. This gives visibility into where files are being used and whether they were accessed, shared, or reviewed.

This supports:
- accountability
- better security monitoring
- trust and transparency
- audit-friendly record handling

### 2.11 Asset and Activity Tracking
The platform also tracks the lifecycle of documents and assets. This includes user actions, file history, activity records, and identity-linked document movement.

This is important for:
- risk detection
- document verification
- storing evidence of ownership
- audit tracking

### 2.12 DNA Generation and Advanced Identity Layering
One of the advanced features of the system is DNA generation, which creates a digital identity fingerprint of the uploaded content or asset. The app is designed around multiple identity layers to ensure content can be traced and verified.

This includes:
- generation of digital identity records
- layered protection architecture
- risk-aware tracking
- uniqueness of stored data
- forensic traceability of content ownership

### 2.13 15-Layer DNA Protection Concept
The system is built around the idea of multi-layer identity protection, where data is represented through multiple layers of verification, encryption, and uniqueness checks. This helps create a strong digital fingerprint and makes unauthorized copying, tampering, or misuse harder.

The DNA layer concept supports:
- secure asset identity creation
- file integrity checks
- tamper detection
- content tracing
- verification-based access control

### 2.14 Web/Cloud Monitoring Support
The app also includes tracking and monitoring of assets beyond local access. This means users can keep an eye on whether content appears in suspicious or public locations, helping reduce leaks and digital misuse.
